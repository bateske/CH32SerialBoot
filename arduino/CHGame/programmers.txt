# putting the CHGame bootloader onto a board that does not have it yet, or
# rescuing one whose bootloader was damaged. Normal uploads never come here.
#

# built into the chip, entered by holding BOOT across a power cycle. Nothing
# needs to be plugged in beyond the USB cable.
